Santiago — Concept Review, Round 2

Two directions, built from what you liked.

Round 1's strongest bits — the cursor-reactive glow and cinematic intro from Fog Gate, the smoothness and elegance of Atelier Noir — feed straight into these two. Both now open on a real 3D grand piano you can hear play one of three recital pieces. One is built entirely around scroll spectacle and motion; the other is quieter and more refined — walking into a theatre for a recital.

01 — Motion

Motion

Scroll spectacle, drastic scene changes, a minigame — motion and visual quality are the whole point.

Fixes round 1's rough scroll and empty stretches: buttery Lenis-driven pacing, dramatic per-section transformations, and at least one polished interactive minigame, without sacrificing animation quality anywhere.

↗ 02 — Recital

Recital

Entering a theatre, taking a seat, watching a piano recital — refined, elegant, ML-aware.

Continues what worked best in Atelier Noir: restraint, smoothness, quality typography. The 3D grand piano sits centre-stage under a spotlight; ML projects are framed as the evening's programme.
